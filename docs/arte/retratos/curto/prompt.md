# Retrato de frente — corte Curto (imagem-base)

**Usado em:** criação do jogador, apresentação no clube e cartão final.

**Arquivo da imagem:** salve nesta pasta como `imagem.jpeg`. Os outros cinco cortes são edições desta imagem.

## Prompt

```
Create a vertical 4:5 portrait image.

A semi-realistic painted digital illustration for a mobile football career game. Painterly look: soft blended shading, visible brush texture, gentle gradients of light and shadow, and only very thin, subtle linework. Realistic human proportions and anatomy. Not a cartoon, comic, cel-shaded art, flat vector art, anime, 3D render or photograph.

A young Brazilian football player shown from the waist up, facing the camera directly, standing straight with his arms relaxed at his sides and a calm, confident expression with a closed mouth. He has brown skin, dark-brown eyes, no beard and short curly dark-brown hair, cut close to the head.

He wears a plain, pure bright magenta short-sleeved football shirt with a simple round collar. This exact flat colour is essential: software will later replace the magenta with each club's colours. The shirt is completely blank: no crest, no logo, no number, no text, no stripes.

The background is a single flat, uniform, pure bright green colour with no gradient, no shadow and no objects, because the character will be cut out by software. Nothing else in the image may be magenta or green. Soft, even studio lighting from the front.

The image must contain no text, letters or numbers, no logos, crests, badges or brand marks, no watermark or signature, and no frames or borders. He is an original fictional character who does not resemble any real person.
```

## Conferência

- [ ] De frente, da cintura para cima, mesmo rosto do retrato-base
- [ ] Camisa magenta lisa, sem escudo, número ou texto
- [ ] Fundo verde chapado, sem sombra nem objetos
- [ ] Sem marca d'água
