# Cartões do Over — as 6 artes de uma vez

**O que é:** o fundo do cartão do Over (o quadradinho com "OVR" e o número), um por faixa de overall. O número e a sigla "OVR" **não** vão na imagem: o jogo escreve por cima.

**Usada em:** ficha do jogador na tela de decisão e, depois, no cartão final.

**Como usar**
1. Abra uma conversa **nova** no Gemini.
2. Cole tudo o que está dentro do bloco "Prompt" e envie. Não anexe nenhuma imagem.
3. Se ele parar antes da imagem 6, responda `Continue with the next image.`
4. Salve cada imagem **nesta pasta** (`docs/arte/cartoes-over/`) com o nome da tabela.

**Onde salvar cada imagem**

| # | Arquivo | Cartão | Faixa de overall |
|--:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `bronze.jpeg` | Bronze | até 49 (Fraco) |
| 2 | `prata.jpeg` | Prata | 50 a 64 (Regular) |
| 3 | `ouro.jpeg` | Ouro | 65 a 74 (Bom) |
| 4 | `platina.jpeg` | Platina | 75 a 84 (Muito bom) |
| 5 | `esmeralda.jpeg` | Esmeralda | 85 a 94 (Excelente) |
| 6 | `diamante.jpeg` | Diamante | 95 a 99 (Lendário) |

## Prompt

````
You will generate 6 SEPARATE images in this conversation. Start from a clean slate: ignore everything from any earlier conversation.

WHAT THIS JOB IS
Six background plates for the rating card of a football career game. All six are the same card in six different materials, from the least valuable to the most valuable: bronze, silver, gold, platinum, emerald, violet diamond. Software writes the rating number on top afterwards.

RULES FOR THE WHOLE JOB
1. Generate exactly 6 images, one for each prompt below, in the order given: image 1, then image 2, and so on up to image 6.
2. Each image is a separate, complete, full-size SQUARE picture showing ONE card face. Never combine them: no grid, no row of cards, no collage, no comparison sheet.
3. The six cards form one family: the same square format, the same rim width, the same direction of light (upper left), the same calm empty centre. ONLY the material and its colour change.
4. Each card must look more precious than the one before it.
5. Do not write any text, label or number inside any image.
6. Before each image, write one short line outside the image with its number and file name, exactly as given in its heading.
7. If you cannot generate all 6 in one reply, generate as many as you can and stop. When I write "Continue with the next image.", carry on from the next number. Never start again from image 1.

=============== IMAGE 1 OF 6 — file name: bronze.jpeg ===============
Create a square 1:1 image at the highest resolution available (about 2048 x 2048 pixels).

SUBJECT
The blank face of one collectible rating card for a football game, seen perfectly flat and straight-on, filling the ENTIRE image from edge to edge. It is only the card's background material: an empty plate waiting for a number.

MATERIAL AND COLOUR
The card is made of warm bronze: reddish-brown metal with orange-copper highlights, like a worn bronze medal. Main colours #D9A070 (light) and #B87A48 (dark), darker rim #7A4A24.
A brushed bronze plate. The surface has fine horizontal brush marks and a slightly uneven, hand-cast look.

LAYOUT OF THE CARD FACE
- The card face fills the whole square. There is no background around it, no table, no shadow outside it, no perspective and no tilt.
- A thin bevelled rim, about 4% of the width, runs along all four edges, in the darker rim colour, with a fine bright line on its inner edge.
- The corners of the image are square. Do not round the corners and do not leave transparent or white corners: software will round them afterwards.
- The central area, about 70% of the width and height, is calm and evenly lit, with only the subtle texture described above. It must stay empty: software will write a large number there.
- Light comes from the upper left: the upper-left area is the lightest and the lower-right the darkest, as a smooth gradient from the light colour to the dark colour.
- One soft, narrow highlight crosses the upper-left corner diagonally. No lens flare and no glow outside the card.

STYLE
Semi-realistic painted game asset: believable material, soft painted shading, clean edges. It should look like a premium collectible card, not a photograph, not a flat vector icon and not a 3D render with heavy reflections.

MUST NOT APPEAR
- No text, letters or numbers anywhere. No "OVR", no rating, no name.
- No logos, crests, badges, stars, crowns, laurels, shields, flags or brand marks.
- No player, no face, no silhouette, no ball and no trophy.
- No frame within a frame, no ribbon, no banner and no icon.
- No watermark or signature.
````

## Conferência (nota para revisão humana)

- [ ] 6 imagens separadas, quadradas (1:1), na resolução maior
- [ ] A face do cartão ocupa a imagem inteira, de ponta a ponta, sem fundo em volta e sem cantos arredondados
- [ ] Centro calmo e vazio, com espaço para o número
- [ ] Nenhum texto, número, "OVR", estrela, escudo ou marca
- [ ] As seis parecem da mesma família: mesma borda, mesma luz vindo do alto à esquerda
- [ ] Cada uma parece mais valiosa que a anterior; prata e platina não se confundem
- [ ] O número em tinta escura continua legível por cima (no diamante, o número é branco)
