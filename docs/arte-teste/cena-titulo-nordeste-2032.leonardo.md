# Cena do título (Maestro, Copa do Nordeste 2032) — versão para o Leonardo.ai

## Prompt

Semi-realistic painted digital illustration, soft painterly shading, subtle brush texture, thin clean linework, realistic proportions, cinematic night lighting. Back view of a young Brazilian football midfielder standing on the pitch, face not visible, brown skin, short curly dark hair, athletic build. His left arm is raised straight up, index finger pointing to the night sky, in a quiet emotional celebration. He wears a completely plain royal-blue shirt with red collar and red cuffs, plain red shorts, royal-blue socks. The back of his shirt is a smooth, empty, evenly lit blue surface. In the blurred distance, team-mates in the same blue-and-red kit hug each other beside a gold trophy cup with two handles on a small podium. Softly blurred crowd in the stands waving plain solid-colour blue and red flags, blue and red confetti falling through floodlight beams. Stadium at night in north-eastern Brazil. Limited palette: deep navy blue night and shadows, pitch green, concrete grey, off-white highlights, a single golden-yellow accent on the trophy and floodlights. Vertical composition: the player and all the action in the upper part of the frame; the bottom third is empty dark grass fading into deep navy shadow.
